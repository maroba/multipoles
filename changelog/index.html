    Changelog

    All notable changes to multipoles are documented here. The format is based on Keep a Changelog.

    Unreleased

    0.5.0 - 2026-10-22

    Added

    • MultipoleExpansion.field(x, y, z): the field E = -∇Φ, computed analytically.
    • center argument to expand about any point instead of the center of charge. This makes interior expansions possible where the center of charge coincides with a charge.
    • validity_radius and is_valid(x, y, z): where the expansion converges.
    • ExpansionValidityWarning when evaluating the expansion where it does not converge, and the charge_tol argument to control how much of a continuous charge density may be neglected for this check.
    • dipole_moment and quadrupole_moment: the cartesian dipole vector and traceless quadrupole tensor.
    • total_charge for discrete charge distributions (previously only for continuous ones).
    • multipole_moments_array: the multipole moments as array, indexed by [l, m].
    • A readable repr, type hints for the public API, and ExpansionValidityWarning, InvalidChargeDistributionException and InvalidExpansionException exported from multipoles.

    Changed

    • Much faster: computing the moments of a 101³ grid takes 0.17 s instead of 2.4 s for l_max=4, and 0.3 s instead of 24 s for l_max=10. Evaluating the expansion is about 20 times faster.
    • Evaluating the potential at points inside the charge distribution (exterior expansion) or outside of it (interior expansion) now emits an ExpansionValidityWarning. This includes evaluating on the whole grid with Phi[:, :, :]. The results there were never meaningful.
    • total_charge of continuous charge distributions is now integrated with Simpson's rule, consistent with the multipole moments, instead of a plain sum over the grid.
    • multipoles no longer uses scipy.special for the spherical harmonics.

    0.4.2 - 2026-10-22

    Fixed

    • Import error with SciPy >= 1.17, where scipy.special.sph_harm was removed.
    • Phi(x, y, z, l_max=...) ignored l_max.
    • exterior=False alone selected the exterior instead of the interior expansion.
    • Phi[...] used the exterior expansion for interior expansions and modified internal state.
    • Evaluating Phi[...] for discrete charges failed with an AttributeError; it now raises a clear TypeError.
    • Invalid input now raises errors instead of silently producing NaN: zero total charge, charge at the center of an interior expansion, grids from np.meshgrid without indexing='ij', and mismatched grid shapes.
    • Grids with descending coordinates gave wrong results.
    • Evaluation accepts arrays of any shape, including 0-d arrays, and is vectorized.

    Changed

    • Requires Python >= 3.9.
